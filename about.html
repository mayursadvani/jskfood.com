About Us

JSK Enterprises Foods, established in the year 2017, is a Distributor of Chocolate & Cocoa, Bakery Ingredients, Sauces & Dressings, Frozen Snacks and more — serving the Bakery, Confectionery, QSR and Hotel Industry with quality raw materials.

The solid product range we offer is sourced from reputed vendors for unmatched qualities such as being chemical-free, good in taste, pure and rich in smell. We take bulk orders of these products, made possible by our spacious warehousing unit, which is divided into various segments to systematically store our products. Our offered products are available at an affordable price range, and we ensure they are delivered to our clients on time.
