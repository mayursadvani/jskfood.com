Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary by product and brand. Get in touch with us on WhatsApp or by phone and we'll confirm MOQs for the items you need.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver daily across Goa, from Canacona in the south to Pernem in the north, with our own dedicated fleet. Major cities like Panjim, Madgao and Mapusa are served on day-wise, alternate-day routes.

How do I place an order?

You can reach us by phone, email or WhatsApp. Our sales teams in North Goa and South Goa can also visit you directly to take orders and understand your requirements.

What are your payment terms?

Payment terms are discussed and agreed with each customer directly — contact us to know what works best for your business.

Do you supply to businesses outside the Bakery/Horeca industry?

We primarily serve the Bakery, Confectionery, QSR, Hotel and Beverage industries, but reach out if you have a different requirement — we're happy to see if we can help.

Can I get product samples before ordering in bulk?

In many cases, yes. Contact us with the specific products you're interested in and we'll let you know what's possible.
